      How it works

      1. Connect. Kukai social login or any Tezos wallet — sixty seconds, no seed-phrase drama. Your address becomes your passport.
      2. Earn stamps. Soulbound tokens for things you actually did. Open stamps claim right here; signed ones take a voucher from the issuer. They can never be bought, sold, or moved.
      3. Mint your noun. Hold First Steps and the office lets you mint a portrait — CC0 pixel art stored entirely on-chain, accessories earned from your stamps.
      4. Mail postcards. Send a noun to a friend with a public note that rides the same confirmation. It hangs on their wall forever.
      5. Cast. Every passport has a broadcast tower: short notes, append-only, no edit, no delete, no admin. The wire below belongs to everyone, forever.
      6. Print your page. Every passport gets a public one-sheet — portrait, links, stamp strip — at one address, yours to share. Links are casts, so the page is just the latest reading of a permanent log. Works with your .tez name, folds into a QR code, saves as a contact card, embeds anywhere.
