the fountain — toss a coin, make a wish

One coin joins today’s circle. One wallet receives one equal share, however many coins it tosses; every extra coin waters everyone. After the clock ends, anyone may close the day, then each wallet claims independently. No house, no chance — a mirror with a delay.

reading the water…

Public forever: your wish, wallet address, and transaction will be readable on Tezos. Leave it blank for a silent coin.

Risk notice: this is experimental, unaudited money-moving code. Calls are public and irreversible, and tez can be lost or remain locked. This is not an investment or a guaranteed return; use only what you can afford to lose.

The first coin starts the first full-length day. Closing is manual: the clock alone moves no money. A late toss closes an expired day automatically and joins the next one. Closing fixes the shares but pays nobody; each wallet claims its own share later. Claims do not expire. If a wallet loses access before claiming, its share stays reserved forever; no admin can recover it. Each signed operation has a normal Tezos network fee.

Many-wallet risk: a “wallet” means one Tezos address, not one person. Someone can use many funded addresses to take many shares, including value added by repeat coins from other wallets. The fountain does not prevent this. Watch the live wallet count before joining.
